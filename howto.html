How it works

Click the button to select characters.

⚠️The order doesn’t matter.⚠️

⚠️Set the star level (currently works only for ULTRA).⚠️

The top 3 best equipment will appear after selecting a character.

The suggested equipment takes into account the presence of other selected characters.

Click the arrows to switch to weaker equipment.
⚠️Not all characters and equipment are added yet.⚠️
⚠️This website is created solely to help players find the best equipment for their teams.⚠️
⚠️If you notice a bug, please take a screenshot and submit a report with a description of the issue using the forms at the top.⚠️
⚠️The site is regularly updated to stay as helpful as possible.⚠️

I wish you good luck and enjoyable gameplay!
